# NEXUS
## Background
Nexus is a web app created to give students an easy way to converse and study with their classmates without the hassle of obtaining contact information. It aims to make communication and studying easier for classmates by providing a consolidated platform for study tools. We want students to be able to collaborate on notes, calculate and predict their grades, and talk to each other without having to obtain contact information. Nexus automatically puts students into their class's Discord channel (created from the classes in the student’s Blackboard), has a grade calculator, and offers a Superdoc: a master document containing uploaded notes from all students in a class, with redundant information removed by AI. It was originally created as part of ACM Projects in Fall 2024, going on to be the semester’s winner.
